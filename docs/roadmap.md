## Out of scope (per PRD §42)
The PRD explicitly excludes: microservices, Kubernetes, Redis, message queues, event-driven architecture, multiple databases, cloud infrastructure, OAuth providers, payment systems, email notifications, advanced analytics, AI features, complex certificate verification, enterprise permission management. None of these are on the roadmap.
